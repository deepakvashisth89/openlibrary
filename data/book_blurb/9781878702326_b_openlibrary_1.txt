When you need to run away from your crazy life, a tropical island is not a bad place to start.

Kelly Sanders needs to get away. Her parents insist on attempting to rule her life. Her possessive boyfriend is trying to trick her into marriage. And her job demands 90% of her time. When her high-spirited grandmother asks her to leave it all behind and join her on a cruise to the Bahamas, Kelly doesn’t think twice.

When the cruise ship breaks down outside Nassau, Kelly and her grandmother decide to finish off their vacation on Harbour Island. Grandma Edie likes the peace and quiet, and Kelly likes the local sights—namely Mike Kramer, owner of a chain of local dive shops. With sun-bleached hair and Caribbean-blue eyes, he doesn’t seem to have a care in the world. But can Kelly go from a life of too much responsibility to a relationship with someone she thinks is completely lacking in it?
