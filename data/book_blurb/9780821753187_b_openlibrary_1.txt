Beloved #3
In 1340, the tyrannical English King Edward turns Scotland into a place of terror and oppression, forcing Anne Faurer from her ancestral home and into servitude on her own lands. She knows little of the new laird of Langlannoch--only that he is English and her sworn enemy. Byard Berkeley knows nothing of Anne's true identiry--only that he desires her, and as Scotland rises from the ashes, a magnificent, enduring love awaits them.
