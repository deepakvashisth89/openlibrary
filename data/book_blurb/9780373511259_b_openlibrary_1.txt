Dr. Harry Jones is pretty sure that it is a mistake having Rebecca Irwin work in their Southshore practice. As daughter of his partner, surely her father will make allowances he wouldn’t with someone else? Unfortunately for Harry, Rebecca overhears their conversation, and despite the raging attraction, between her and Harry she doesn’t hesitate to fight her corner!

Will they ever manage to get some balance into their roller-coaster feelings, and sort out what they really do want from each other?
