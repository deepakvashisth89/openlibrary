SEVEN YEARS STOOD BETWEEN THEM...

Seven years since Lani's love for her aristocratic stepbrother, Daniel, had turned to anger and cold dislike.

Now he was back--to steal away the small brother she had raised. He gave her an ultimatum: accompany them to Hawaii or lose her only remaining family.

She went to Hawaii, but nothing had prepared her for what she would face. Daniel teased her with kisses and scorched her with anger, tempted her with promised delights and sent her away with mocking smiles.
/> Why had he brought her here only to drive her away?
