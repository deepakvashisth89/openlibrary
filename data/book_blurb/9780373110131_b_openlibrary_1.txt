She knew David was the man for her.

It didn't take long for artist Liz Redwood to fall in love with the English baronet David Warren Castle. She'd always admired his paintings. And when circumstances brought them together for the summer at the lavish Villa Delphini in Portofino, she discovered him to be a tender, considerate man--the passionate lover she thought could exist only in her dreams.

Liz was ready to commit herself to David forever.

Until she learned of Bethany—the woman who'd apparently already claimed his heart.
