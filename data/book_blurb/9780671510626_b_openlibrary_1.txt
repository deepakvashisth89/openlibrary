A castle, a cayman, a coachload of tourists, and a couple of suspicious deaths. Superintendent Robert Bone is on the scene, but he is also on his honeymoon, and out of his jurisdiction. 
Will he be able to help solve the mysteries, unofficially and without treading on the toes of the local Inspector? 
Robert and Grizel are not Lord and Lady Peter Wimsey, but this is a delightful honeymoon whodunit set at a castle in Kent.
