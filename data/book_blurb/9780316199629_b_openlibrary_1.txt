A collection of eight short stories:
 1. The Prophet from Jupiter
 2. Charlotte (selected for The Best American Short Stories 1993 and New Stories from the South: The Year's Best 1993)
 3. Here We Are in Paradise
 4. Gettysburg
 5. Lord Randall
 6. Aliceville (selected by National Public Radio's "Sound of Writing" as one of the best stories of 1992)
 7. Story of Pictures
 8. My Father's Heart (named a Distinguished Story of 1992 in The Best American Short Stories)
