They were the family Lyon. Strong. Sure. Gifted. Cursed...

They sailed from embattled England to America's shores, aristocratic exiles in the land of the common man. But not even an ocean could heal the breach of a house divided against itself, a family riven by hate.

Rebeckah, the headstrong beauty, following her heart to it's meeting with destiny in a majestic and lonely place... Amos, playing God with his family's future, until life played its cruelest trick... Benn, possessed by the guilty image of a ravaged mother that would haunt him to his dying day... Bruce, giving his heart to Rebeckah only to lose her to another man -- until desire brought them together again.
