For ten years, beautiful golden girl Cyndi Saint had been running... trying to escape the haunting memories of the night her carefree world had exploded into devastating tragedy. Then she met brash, irresistible Nick Calderaro. Tenderly, insistently, he awoke her dormant passions and dared her to dream again. With his help, could she banish the ghosts of the past and open her heart to love?
