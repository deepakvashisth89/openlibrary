**There was a sleeping tiger behind those golden eyes — a tiger getting ready to pounce...**

Lovely American Jacinth Essex came to France to learn the perfume business at the luxurious estate of the St. Amours, one of the great perfume houses of France. The family made her feel very welcome, and the unexpected advances of Lorenz, a nephew of the St. Amours, stirred her almost against her will. Lorenz was a most attractive Man, and he wanted Jacinth from the moment he saw her. Jacinth didn't know whether she loved or hated him. But suddenly she was forced to decide...
