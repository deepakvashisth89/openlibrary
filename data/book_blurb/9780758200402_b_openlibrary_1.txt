***Book #3 in the Robert Wilsop and Friends Mystery series***

Fasten your seat belts because David Stukas, author of the riotously fun *Someone Killed His Boyfriend* and *Going Down for the Count*, is back with another wickedly entertaining outing featuring accidental sleuths Michael, Robert, and Monette. This time, the trio are off to Palm Springs, where a rivalry between the world-famous White Party and the upstart Red Party is sizzling, turning the hottest circuit in town into a festival of murder.

Mama said there'd be days like this. That's the refrain running through Robert's modest, Midwestern mind as he and Michael are driven to the home of party promoter Rex Gifford by the mogul's naked manservant, Vince. Never mind that the guy is sporting enough hardware in his not-so-software to be a human wind chime. Or that Michael's managed to join the mile-high club on the way down. Or that Rex's plan for a Red Party to rival the celebrated White Party has started an all-out war among the gay mafia controlling the circuit party world. At least the pool is heated.
