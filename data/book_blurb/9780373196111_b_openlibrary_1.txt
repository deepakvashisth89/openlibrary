Synopsis - Her assignment with the boss!  By day, Julie Fasano was secretary to dashing private investigator Hunter Matthews.  By nigh, she was The Mystery Kisser a journalist assigned to kiss 100 men and report on the results!  There was just one problem – Hunter’s lasts assignment was to discover the identity of the mystery kisser, and he wanted Julie to help.  She was stuck; she hated lying to Hunter because she was swiftly losing her heart to him.  The solution? Hunter had to become the first man on her kissing hit list – and with just one kiss Julie was hoping to hook him for life...
