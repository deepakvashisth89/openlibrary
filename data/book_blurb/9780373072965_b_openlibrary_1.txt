He Was Her Last Chance . . . 

For her son, Glynis Hamilton would do anything — even confront the man who'd broken her heart years before. Once Glynis had loved Cort McBride, but, consumed by ambition, he'd left town without ever learning of the child they'd created. And now he was her only hope . . .

Now a successful rancher as well as a top-notch security expert, Cort was as dangerously driven as ever. But what frightened Glynis most was her fiery passion for him, which neither time nor distance could quench. He was still the one man who could stir her to dizzying heights of desire, the one man who could make her wildest dreams a reality . . . the one man who could save their son . . .
