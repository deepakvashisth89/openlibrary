WE CALL IT NIGHTMARE...THEY CALL IT HOME
Yesterday John Caldwell was a man. Today he is an experiment--controlled by a power from beyond the stars.
In a desolate sector of the American Southwest, two impenetrable black boxes contain secrets of the Ral--a warrior race of dreams and the mind ... an insatiable breed of parasites who have, at long last, found their host.
John Caldwell is afraid. Once he was human. Tomorrow he will be something different--the ultimate threat to the survival of his species.
