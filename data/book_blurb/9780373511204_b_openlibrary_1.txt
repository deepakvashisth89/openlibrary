He wanted a baby, not a lover.

Tony Austin had no desire for a wife, but he longed for a baby of his own. What he needed was someone to carry the child and then vanish from his life.

She wanted a lover, not a baby.

The last thing Olivia Anderson wanted was a husband ... and a child. All she was expecting was a night or two of incredibly hot, passionate sex.

It should have been perfect...

Scandalous, but perfect. Only sometimes passion gets out of control, and love upsets even the best-laid plans.
