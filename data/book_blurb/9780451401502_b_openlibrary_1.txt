The roaring Yukon in the human floodtide of the gold rush was no proper place for an innocent young lady, especially one as temptingly beautiful as redheaded Kate O'Riley. 

Yet Kate was determined to be with her father there, despite all he could say to warn her off. She thought she was prepared to withstand all perils on this savage frontier, from the fiery lusts of men to the freezing forces of nature. But when fate made her the wife-in-name-only to handsome, danger-loving Hogan O'Shea, she found that a woman's desires could match a man's . . . and that burning passion could turn an ice kingdom of gold into a dazzling realm of love . . .
