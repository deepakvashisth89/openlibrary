She was the most exasperating little lady he'd ever set eyes on. Charlotte Beauchamp, with her mile-a-minute mouth and engaging enthusiasm, had whirled into Tex Brubaker's life like a wild Texas tornado. It had begun when she inherited a million-dollar pig, a porkly problem that animal behaviorist Tex agreed to take on. But dealing with the precocious, pretty heiress was another matter entirely. The Brubaker bachelor knew he'd better watch his back, or he'd find himself agreeing to so much more....
