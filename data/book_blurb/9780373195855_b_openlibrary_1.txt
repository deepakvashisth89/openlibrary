Sheriff Fletcher Harris didn't know how a little girl had ended up at the bus stop, lost and alone, with his name pinned to her shirt, but he damn sure meant to protect her. And he knew only one person with enough love in her heart to help him -- the wife he'd once loved...and let get away.

The beautiful child was a heartbreaking reminder of everything Amanda Harris had lost. But little Shelby also seemed to bring out a new, devastatingly tender side of 34 year old Fletcher -- a side 32 year old Amanda could hardly resist. Could it be this little girl lost could help a husband and wife let go of the past and find their heart's desire once more?
