When three reluctant brides...fall in love with three irresistible grooms...the only words left to say are..."I Do". When a twist of fate brings Anne Moore back together with childhood crush Jud Cammeron, she vows to forget the wild emotions he once aroused. She's a grown woman with a fiance now-but every moment spent with Jud makes her wonder if she's about to walk down the aisle with the wrong man. -- Tina Merritt is determined to make something of her life, despite a few painful setbacks. But sheer ambition won't be enough-not when stubborn and sexy Dirk Tanger controls her trust fund. A financial advance is all she needs to start over, but Dirk won't let her have it unless she accepts his offer of marriage. -- Dedicating herself to her career, independent Tara Schmitt has resolved not to repeat her mother's mistakes. And certainly not with an arrogant man like Alek Rykovsky, who assumes his money and good looks will immediately make her hear weddings bells. He may have everything most women want, but Alek is about to find out that Tara isn't just any woman-and that in the game of love, she makes her own rules.
