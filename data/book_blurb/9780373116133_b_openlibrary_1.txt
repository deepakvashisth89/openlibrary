Though her sister insists the perfect Christmas gift comes in size six foot two, Jodi doubts that Mr. Right is packed in Santa's sleigh this year. With a thriving business and all the fun of being a doting aunt, she's decided that love--with all its insecurity, jealousy and heartache can wait...indefinitely.
David Griffiths, a man who thinks Santa already delivered, has other ideas. He's determined to prove to Jodi that his love isn't seasonal. He's here to stay... all year round.
