Worlds apart--in all but love

What was wrong with her? Moira Tennant was at the pinnacle of her modeling career in a place where it mattered... New York! Yet something vital was missing in her life--until she met Patrick Casey.

"You're glossy, smart and expensive, "the famous playwright informed her, "but only half-real." Patrick could make her feel alive again, ignite her body as no one had before, make her laugh and make her cry.

But what was the point in falling in love when they couldn't even agree on a suitable place to live?
