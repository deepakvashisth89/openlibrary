The Price of deceit...
It had been twelve years ago; Felicia had been only a child. No wonder Joshua Tagget doesn't recognise her! But Felicia remembers him and how he betrayed her stepsister. And now, by making his attraction to Felicia more than clear, Joshua had unwittingly given her the means to pay him back in kind for his deceit. But Felicia isn't immune to Joshua's charms, and she could find it's not only Joshua she's deceiving if she thinks she can walk away from him with her heart intact....
