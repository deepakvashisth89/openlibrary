MR. OCTOBER

Name: Kyle Phillips Occupation: Cowboy--and reluctant hero

Damsel in distress: Lauren Davie

Kyle had seen every manner of occurrence on his TEXAS ranch--even a freak snowstorm couldn't rattle him. But the pretty young thing he'd found snowbound on his property was making the upright bachelor reel with ungentlemanly thoughts. Of all the luck to be stranded with such an innocent beauty ....

Trapped for three days in close quarters, Kyle could feel his temperature rising. His pure-as-the-driven-snow houseguest seemed intent on making him her first conquest! What was a proper cowboy to do?

MAN OF THE MONTH: He's snowbound with an "angel" -- who's a temptress in disguise!
