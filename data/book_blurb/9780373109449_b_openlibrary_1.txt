He might be the puppet master... But Anna was no willing marionette. She was a woman with flesh and blood and feelings.

She found it hard to believe that Cristian Casas might use her to repay her brother-in-law for starting up his own Mediterranean restaurant after quitting his job as Cristian's star chef. Or that Cristian would lavish attention on her to make his ex-lover, the haughty Azucena, jealous.

One thing was certain. Whatever the reason, Cristian did intend to seduce Anna. She could feel the tugging at her heartstrings.
