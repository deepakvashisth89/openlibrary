Dr. Theo Tithonus is a plastic surgeon in Philadelphia whose perfect life is about to come undone. He has a lucrative but time-consuming practice, a neglected yet faithful lover, complicated family drama, and - through artificial insemination with his medical partner - a five-year-old daughter who is a big secret from everyone.

Theo's structured daily routine is shattered when his daughter is deposited at his doorstep one night by her California-bound mother, his lover walks out on him with a challenging ultimatum, his family divides in resentful battle, and political shenanigans at the hospital lead to intrigue and scandal. Wings in the Snow is a novel of choices, second chances, and unexpected joys.
