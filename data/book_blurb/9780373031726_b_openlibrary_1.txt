It was Coffee's move

 And for once, Coffee Dugan didn't hesitate--she knew exactly what she had to do to protect her son. She'd keep him from spending time with Dodge Phillips.

 It was the only way she could stop Dodge from playing chess with Jeffie--and from dredging up memories of the boy's dead father. Memories Coffee would rather forget.

 But can Coffee keep Dodge from capturing her heart?
