Warm, passionate and desperate for love.
Summer Roberts was shy and insecure, denied normal loving relationships by a lifelong battle with her weight. Like the fan-shaped lion’s paw shell, Summer’s fragile confidence could so easily be shattered.
Computer tycoon James Gardiner's entry into her sheltered world was devastating in more ways than one - through his kindness and unintentional cruelty she emerged a slender, beautiful woman, sure of herself and sure of her love....
