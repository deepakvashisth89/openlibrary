SHE HATED THE RUDE GUNSLINGER ALL DAY -- BUT WANTED HIM ALL NIGHT...

DEFIANT SPITFIRE 
Rugged Linc Scott had hired on at Eden Campbell's ranch to protect it from cattle-rustling landgrabbers, but he soon realized that Eden Campbell herself was his biggest problem. The pretty little spitfire refused to let Linc guard her, arguing that she could take care of herself. To show her just how vulnerable she was, Linc wrestled Eden to the ground. But his plan backfired when their close encounter led to a heated kiss... and then to an even hotter embrace. Soon Eden was in more danger of losing her innocence than she was of losing her land...


HIRED GUN
Used to looking after herself, proud Eden Campbell didn't cotton much to the idea of having Linc Scott around all the time. If it weren't for those thieving rustlers the stubborn beauty would never have had to deal with being bossed around by a rude gunslinger. But when she found herself pinned to the ground beneath Linc's lean, muscled body and subject to the assault of his surprisingly sensual lips, she no longer minded his overbearing ways. She'd let him order her around all day, as long as he loved her all night...
