Fearlessly taking aim at the myths, mysteries, arcana, and boondoggles that remain virtually unknown even to military buffs, Dirty Little Secrets of World War II remembers the big one. Like its successful predecessor, Dirty Little Secrets, Dunnigan and Nofi's new book exposes the dark, irreverent, misunderstood, and often tragicomic aspects of military operations. All theaters, all participants, and all forms of warfare are vividly brought to life in more than three hundred infobytes.
