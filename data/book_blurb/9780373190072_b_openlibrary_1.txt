Future Daddy — Shane Michaels -- a successful businessman who had it all. Until, out of the blue, came the nagging sensation that something was missing. Then a strange impulse compelled him to visit his boyhood home.... 

Future Mommy — Jeannie Harrigan -- a country doctor, content with her simple, single life-style. Until a chance meeting with a handsome stranger made her feel that everything was about to change....

Future Baby - baby-in-waiting had chosen his perfect parents. Now, with a little help from above, Jeannie and Shane were about to meet their romantic destiny.
