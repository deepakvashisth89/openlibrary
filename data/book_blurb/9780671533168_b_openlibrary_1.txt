Julie hoped she would find love.

...at her new home in Tall Pines. She looked forward to meeting new friends, learning to ski at her parents' resort, and spending time with her mother after years of separation.

Then Nolan Radford, a performer at the lodge, sang his way into Julie's dreams. Nolan was older than any guy she'd known, but his romantic style made Julie melt inside.

However, Julie found herself spending more time with Scott Gerard, a high school student. Though Scott was not as sophisticated as Nolan, his easy warmth and caring was wonderful. And she knew that in him she had finally found someone special.
