He asked her for one small favor. It involved wearing a white dress... 

Zoe Martin couldn't believe it. She'd waited years for Brad Kenneally to see her as more than the widow he'd promised to watch over. And now she was by his side in white -- becoming his pretend wife for two weeks! It was time to take drastic measures... 

Brad's life was out of control. One minute, he was writing a small country lifestyle column, the next, he had to produce the perfect family he'd written about! Now he faced two intimate weeks with the woman he'd sworn never to touch. He vowed to keep his desire a secret -- until his bride asked for one small favor: a real wedding night.
