Have you ever awakened from a dream so delicious you couldn't bear for it to end?

Have you ever gazed into the eyes of a lover and wished he could see your secret desires?

Then come to a place created especially for you by four of the most sensuous romance authors writing today—a place where you can explore your wildest fantasies and fulfill your deepest longings. Contributors include Anne Avery, Phoebe Conn, Sandra Hill and Dara Joy.

A Dance on the Edge by Anne Avery - When interior designer Marlis Jones battles with architect Jack Martin over e-mail, they discover a love powerful enough to blow their circuits.

My One by Dara Joy - When Lois Ed pleads with the cosmos to help her through her hard times, she never expects her cry to be answered—by a hunk of an alien with the wildest sense of passion she's ever experienced

Heart Craving by Sandra Hill - Nicholas DiCello is desperate—his wife, Paula, plans to divorce him. So when a fortune-telling floozy in a flowered dress swears the only way to win back his wife's love is to discover her heart craving, he listens.

Toss the Bouquet by Phoebe Conn - After her boyfriend falls short of the altar, bridal florist Regan Paisley spends her vacation days at the beach alone. Then she meets a seductive, Italian cyclist who pedals his way into her bed—and her heart.
