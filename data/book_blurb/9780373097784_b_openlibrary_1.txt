The life of a country doctor with a teenage daughter was certainly a full one. But widower Beau Dugan never realized what he'd been missing until Clary Parsons came back to Wellsburg. She was prettier, livelier and more intriguing than he remembered, but she was not home to stay. Perhaps he might change her mind... 

When Clary took in all the familiar sights of the old 'burg,' everything seemed about the same, give or take a decade. Everything except Beau Dugan - he was simply breathtaking! Still, she had a new, exciting L.A. life-style. So why did the fast lane suddenly pale in comparison to her sleepy hometown?
