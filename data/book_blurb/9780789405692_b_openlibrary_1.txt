Owners and aficionados alike will enjoy the unique blend of keepsake and reference books in this easy-to-follow, colorfully illustrated series of breed-specific handbooks. Each breed handbook covers every aspect of dog care, including a full profile of each featured breed and information on basic day-to-day care, training, grooming and exercise. With more than 150 color photos, Labrador owners will find this handbook to be an invaluable assets. It is full of information about this particular breed, such as breed variations, characteristics, anatomy, and a behavior profile. Readers learn about choosing the right puppy, caring for and training it, diet, grooming and showing. Each book is illustrated with color photographs that bring the information to life, making it simple to understand at a glance.
