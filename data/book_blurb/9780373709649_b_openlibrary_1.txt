FROM MINISTER TO MOM…

Katherine Blake had long found fulfillment in caring for her family of parishioners, never daring to believe she could have a family all her own—until now. But how could she now turn her back on the motherless Carlson children she'd befriended—or their father? Especially when she thought God had a special reason for bringing them into her life.

Michael Carlson had had his fill of church dogooders when his wife died, but Katherine was different from any woman of faith he'd ever known. Her exuberance and spontaneity made his family come alive again. Still, could this caring female minister persuade Michael to once again open his heart to God—and love…?
